The Story

Hi, I'm Shri.
A healthcare professional
who left the clinic to fix it.

I practiced medicine for a year in India. I saw patients, wrote prescriptions, redesigned a clinic's EHR. And somewhere in that year I noticed something that wouldn't leave me alone.

The most important decisions about my patients weren't made by me. They were made by the systems around me — who gets screened, what gets covered, how data flows between a hospital and an insurer. The doctor is one node in a much larger network. I wanted to understand that network.

So I left clinical practice, moved to St. Louis, and started a dual MPH/MBA at Washington University. Since then I've done cancer survival research at Siteman, built a health‑tech product from zero with AI as a co‑architect, helped a struggling hospital find $24M in opportunities, and presented at ASCO 2026.

The work takes a lot of shapes now — Cox models on 20 years of NHANES, a chronic‑care app built spec‑first with AI in the loop, hospital financial turnarounds, and a locally‑run AI pipeline producing long‑form health video on a single GPU. The method stays the same: spec first, locked decisions, primary sources cited, AI as a co‑builder, never a crutch.

Every project comes back to the same question: how do we make the system work better for the people inside it?

How I Work

I build with AI,
not around it.

Every project starts from a written specification in plain Markdown: research synthesis, thresholds, edge cases, locked decisions, all captured before the first file is written. Code follows the spec, not the other way around.

01

Spec‑driven development

Research, thresholds, and edge cases are locked in a plain Markdown spec before any code is written. Open‑ended prompts produce debt; locked prompts produce verifiable output.

02

Multi‑chat AI orchestration

Parallel AI chats for different phases of a single project — research, architecture, implementation review, prompt drafting. Each chat holds its own context; artifacts feed the others. Decisions stay traceable across the build.

03

Instrumented before expanded

I defer architectural complexity until telemetry justifies it. New data sources, new services, new layers all wait on evidence from the baseline. One step per agentic session, verified on device before the next one starts.

04

Clinical grounding

Health‑domain thresholds come from primary guidelines (ADA, AHA, DASH, NHLBI, WHO, CDC, FDA). Cited inline, evidence grades flagged, edge cases worked — never paraphrased from secondary summaries.

Spec first. Locked decisions. Instrumented before expanded. Verified before advanced.
