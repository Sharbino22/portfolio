Hi, I'm Shri.

Physician turned healthcare strategist. I use epidemiology, data, and strategy to redesign the systems that shape care.

I practiced medicine for a year in India. Saw patients, wrote prescriptions, redesigned a clinic's EHR. And somewhere in that year, I noticed something: the most important decisions about my patients weren't made by me. They were made by the systems around me.

Who gets screened. What gets covered. How data flows between a hospital and an insurer. The doctor is one node in a much larger network. I wanted to understand that network. So I left clinical practice, moved to St. Louis, and started a dual MPH/MBA at Washington University.

Since then, I've worked on cancer survival research at Siteman Cancer Center, built a health tech product from scratch, helped a struggling hospital find $24M in opportunities, and presented at ASCO 2026. Every project comes back to the same question: how do we make the system work better for the people inside it?
